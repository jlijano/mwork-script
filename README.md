# MWORK Call Script Masterclass

This repository documents the MWORK collision-repair outbound call script as a complete operating system for accident-lead conversations.

The source material is not just a word-for-word phone script. It combines:

- live call language,
- customer-empathy guidance,
- repair-shop positioning,
- insurance and claim-routing logic,
- vehicle driveability and safety qualification,
- benefit and credibility talking points,
- objection handling,
- closing concepts,
- voicemail language,
- agent coaching notes,
- and CRM-relevant discovery fields.

The goal of this repository is to turn that dense source material into a clear, teachable, repeatable conversation framework that agents can understand and use naturally.

## Core Conversation Framework

The simplest way to understand the MWORK call is:

**CONNECT → CARE → DISCOVER → QUALIFY → SAFETY → POSITION → SOLVE → HANDLE → CLOSE → DOCUMENT**

### 1. CONNECT
Establish who the customer is, reference the vehicle, identify yourself, and explain the immediate practical reason for the call.

### 2. CARE
Acknowledge that the customer has been through an accident. Check their wellbeing and listen for immediate concerns.

### 3. DISCOVER
Find out where the vehicle is, whether a claim has been filed, whether insurance has contacted them, whether a rental is arranged, and whether they have already selected a repair facility.

### 4. QUALIFY
Confirm that you are speaking with the person responsible for the repair and claim decisions. Determine how the repair will be paid: own insurance, third-party insurance, or self-pay.

### 5. SAFETY
Determine whether the vehicle is driveable and safe. If driveability is uncertain, use the safety-check questions in the source script.

### 6. POSITION
Explain that MWORK is an independent repair facility that works directly with insurance carriers for estimates and payments, while the customer retains control over the repair-facility decision.

### 7. SOLVE
Match the customer's real problem to the most relevant MWORK benefit. Do not dump every feature into the call.

### 8. HANDLE
Treat objections as signals of an underlying concern: cost, trust, convenience, insurer influence, uncertainty, transportation, or switching friction.

### 9. CLOSE
Move the call toward one clear next action: inspection, tow, rental coordination, document exchange, callback, or another agreed step.

### 10. DOCUMENT
Record claim status, payor, vehicle condition, vehicle location, rental need, selected shop, decision-maker status, main concern, and call outcome.

## Most Important Principle

This script should not be trained as a long paragraph agents memorize.

It should be trained as a **decision-based conversation framework**.

The agent's job is to identify where the customer is in the claim, understand the customer's immediate problem, choose the correct next question, present only the relevant value, and secure the next action.

## Source-Script Cautions

The source contains several strong or absolute statements involving insurance coverage, repair-shop choice, OEM parts, rental coverage, timelines, deductible assistance, out-of-pocket cost, and legal rights. Those statements should be reviewed and approved by MWORK's legal/compliance leadership before being treated as mandatory verbatim language.

The source also contains internal contradictions or ambiguous labels, including language such as `SKIP SAFETY QUESTIONS`, while immediately listing vehicle-safety questions. This repository treats those items as workflow-design problems that should be clarified before agent rollout.
