# MWORK Live Call Flow

This document translates the master script into a practical decision tree agents can use during live calls.

## Core Rule

Do not try to recite the entire source script.

The agent should identify the customer's current state and ask the **next best question**.

---

# 1. OPEN

### Objective
Establish relevance immediately.

### Agent tasks
- Confirm the customer.
- Introduce yourself.
- Reference the vehicle.
- Explain that MWORK may be able to help with inspection, repair coordination, towing, or rental if those items are not already arranged.

### Then ask
- Is now an okay time to talk?
- How are you doing after the accident?

If the customer is distressed, injured, busy, or unavailable, stop pushing the script and schedule an appropriate callback.

---

# 2. CONFIRM DECISION-MAKER

Ask whether the person is the one handling the repair and claim decisions.

### If YES
Continue.

### If NO
Identify who is responsible and document the correct contact path.

### If SHARED
Ask who else needs to be involved before a repair decision can be made.

---

# 3. DISCOVER CURRENT STATUS

Use these questions in a natural order.

## Vehicle
- Where is the vehicle right now?
- Was it towed?
- Is it driveable?

## Claim
- Have you filed a claim?
- Which insurance company is handling it?
- Has an adjuster contacted you yet?

## Transportation
- Do you have anything else to drive?
- Has a rental already been arranged?

## Repair facility
- Have you already chosen a repair facility?
- Is the vehicle already at another shop?

## Documentation
- Do you have the crash report?
- Do you have the claim number?
- Has anyone provided an estimate yet?

Do not ask every question if the answer has already been volunteered.

---

# 4. DETERMINE WHO IS PAYING

Route the customer into the correct path.

### Own insurance
Customer is using their own policy.

### Third-party insurance
Customer is pursuing the other driver's carrier.

### Self-pay
No insurance claim is funding the repair.

### Undetermined
Coverage or liability has not yet been established.

The agent should avoid making assumptions about deductible, coverage, rental, or liability before this is clear.

---

# 5. DRIVEABILITY / SAFETY CHECK

If the vehicle is already clearly non-driveable or was towed because of serious damage, proceed toward the non-driveable path.

If driveability is uncertain, ask relevant safety questions:

- Any airbags deployed?
- Any broken windows, mirrors, or glass?
- Are headlights and taillights functioning?
- Is anything leaking?
- Are the wheels and tires safe?
- Are there new warning lights on the dash?
- Is the exhaust bent or hanging?
- Does the vehicle pull to one side?
- Does the hood open and close properly?
- Do all doors open and close properly?
- Does the trunk/liftgate function properly?

### Outcome A — Non-driveable / questionable safety
Move toward towing, pickup, inspection, and transportation coordination.

### Outcome B — Driveable
Move toward inspection/estimate scheduling and transportation planning for repair time.

---

# 6. POSITION MWORK

Only after enough discovery has happened, explain the MWORK model.

### Core positioning
- MWORK is an independent repair facility.
- MWORK works directly with insurance companies for estimates and payments.
- The customer can still choose where the vehicle is repaired.
- MWORK can help coordinate the repair process.

Do not overload the customer with every feature.

---

# 7. MATCH THE RIGHT BENEFIT

Use the customer's problem to choose the benefit.

### If the concern is trust
Use credibility: warranty, certifications, locations, years in business, manufacturer capabilities.

### If the concern is transportation
Use rental/towing coordination where applicable.

### If the concern is repair quality
Use repair capability, certification, manufacturer procedures, warranty, and approved parts language.

### If the concern is convenience
Use coordination, towing/transport, insurance communication, and single-facility capability.

### If the concern is cost
Use only approved language related to insurance coordination, deductible programs, pricing, or financing.

### If the concern is total loss
Use approved total-loss and buyback guidance.

---

# 8. HANDLE OBJECTIONS

Use:

**Acknowledge → Clarify → Answer → Evidence → Question**

Never argue.

Always return to a useful question after answering.

Examples:

### “Insurance told me where to go.”
Acknowledge the guidance they received, explain approved repair-choice language, then ask whether the vehicle is already at that shop.

### “I’ll wait for insurance.”
Ask whether an adjuster has already been assigned and whether inspection has been scheduled.

### “I don’t want to switch shops.”
Clarify whether repairs have begun or whether the car was only dropped off/estimated.

### “I’ve never heard of you.”
Use credibility points relevant to the customer's vehicle and location, then ask what matters most to them when choosing the facility.

---

# 9. CLOSE TO ONE NEXT ACTION

Every productive call should end with a specific operational outcome.

### Inspection
Schedule date/time or defined inspection step.

### Tow / pickup
Confirm vehicle location and approved transport process.

### Rental
Start or coordinate the approved rental process.

### Documents
Request or send the needed claim information, estimate, report, or authorization.

### Callback
Set a specific follow-up time or trigger.

### Declined
Respect the customer's decision and document it.

### Already committed elsewhere
Record current shop and future follow-up possibility.

### Total loss
Move into the approved total-loss workflow.

---

# 10. DOCUMENT

Before completing the call, capture:

- Customer / decision-maker status
- Vehicle year/make/model
- Vehicle location
- Driveability status
- Claim filed: yes/no
- Insurance carrier
- Claim number if available
- Own policy / third-party / self-pay
- Adjuster assigned: yes/no
- Rental status
- Repair facility selected: yes/no
- Current shop if applicable
- Primary customer concern
- Objection(s) raised
- MWORK benefit discussed
- Next action
- Follow-up date/time
- Final disposition

---

# Compact Decision Tree

```text
CONTACT
   ↓
Available to talk?
   ├── No → Schedule callback
   └── Yes
        ↓
Decision-maker?
   ├── No → Identify correct person
   └── Yes
        ↓
Vehicle location / claim status / rental / shop
        ↓
Who is paying?
   ├── Own insurance
   ├── Third-party insurance
   ├── Self-pay
   └── Undetermined
        ↓
Driveable and safe?
   ├── No / uncertain → Tow / pickup / inspect path
   └── Yes → Inspection / estimate path
        ↓
What is the primary customer concern?
        ↓
Present only relevant MWORK benefit
        ↓
Objection?
   ├── Yes → ACAEQ objection framework
   └── No
        ↓
Secure specific next action
        ↓
Document CRM disposition
```

# Agent Reminder

The goal is not to finish the script.

The goal is to correctly diagnose the customer's situation and move them to the right next step.
